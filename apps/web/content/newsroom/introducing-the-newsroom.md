GigaCAD is version control for people who make physical things. Projects have branches you check out, versions you name, and releases that can never be changed. This newsroom is where we'll write about it.

## What you'll find here

- **Essays** on how hardware teams work, and why we think software's best workflow belongs in a hardware shop.
- **Roadmaps** that say where GigaCAD is going. They give direction, not dates, and anything not built yet is marked as planned.
- **News** when something ships: a new feature, a new app, or a change to how GigaCAD works.

## What's here today

You can use GigaCAD in your browser at [app.gigacad.site](https://app.gigacad.site) and from the [command line](/docs/cli). The desktop apps for Windows and Mac are [in development](/download), along with the SolidWorks add-in.

## Following along

Every article is in the [RSS feed](/newsroom/feed.xml), so you can follow the newsroom in any feed reader. The [docs](/docs) cover how GigaCAD works today.
